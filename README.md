 Calm AI - Family

What is it?

This is a simple dashboard I made for parents or guardians to check on someone who has panic attacks.
The idea is simple: during a panic attack, the person might not be able to talk or text.
So how does their parent know if they're okay?

This dashboard shows if the person is safe, in calm mode, or having a panic attack. It also shows alerts and a quick history log.

The project is still in the beginning. It's just a front-end design (HTML and CSS). No AI, no database, no backend yet. Just a simple design to show the idea.

---

Why did I make it?

I was working on Calm AI and thinking about the person having the panic attack. Then I thought... what about their parents?

If someone can't ask for help, their family needs a way to know what's going on. That's why I made this.

---

What's in it right now?

Feature ( What it does )
Current Status : Shows if the person is safe, in calm mode, or having a panic attack 
Alerts : Notifications if something needs attention 
History Log : Quick view of past events 
TimerTrack  : monitoring time
